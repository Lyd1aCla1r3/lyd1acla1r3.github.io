Ebooks

First-principles engineering texts across physical-layer signal integrity and neural network architectures, formatted for long-form reading.

Signal Integrity: From Waves to SerDes

A first-principles exploration of high-speed digital systems across 6 parts and 21 chapters, published in two standalone ebook editions. This series traces electromagnetic wave propagation, S-parameter and jitter measurement, CTLE/DFE/FFE and PAM4 equalization, and SerDes clocking with coherent optics. Volume 1 (Wave and Loss Foundations, Expanded Second Edition) pairs accessible beginner intuition with complete derivations for Chapters 1 to 5. Volume 2 (Measurement, SerDes, and Coherent Optics, Concise First Edition) delivers the concise advanced reference for Chapters 6 to 21.

Tokenization: Byte Pair Encoding

A step-by-step unrolling of the Byte Pair Encoding algorithm. This collection traces the deterministic compression of character-level data into semantic subword units, proving mathematically how the statistical distribution of language generates structural linguistic features before reaching the neural network. (6 chapters)

Vector Embeddings: From Scratch

A complete derivation of end-to-end learned embeddings. This series traces the mathematical progression from discrete token IDs through one-hot encoding, random initialization in high-dimensional space, the forward pass and cross-entropy loss, full backpropagation through both weight matrices, and the emergent semantic geometry that arises from gradient descent at scale. (8 chapters)

Positional Encoding: From Sinusoidal Formulas to Rotary Position Embeddings

A first-principles derivation of positional encoding mechanisms for sequence-aware neural architectures. This series traces the mathematical progression from the order-agnostic embedding tensor through the historical sinusoidal formula, element-wise injection, the query-key dot-product framework, and the full derivation of Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE), the mechanism used in modern language models. (9 chapters)
